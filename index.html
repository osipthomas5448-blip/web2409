Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical roof replacement take?
Most residential roof replacements are completed within 2 to 4 days depending on roof size, complexity, and weather conditions. We provide a specific timeline in your proposal and communicate daily progress updates throughout the project.
Do you assist with insurance claims for storm damage?
Yes. Our team documents all storm damage with drone photography and detailed reports formatted for insurance adjusters. We coordinate directly with your insurance company throughout the claims process at no additional charge.
What roofing materials do you recommend for Colorado's climate?
For most residential properties, we recommend Class 4 impact-resistant architectural shingles which can qualify you for insurance premium discounts. For commercial properties, TPO and EPDM membranes offer excellent UV and thermal performance at altitude.
What warranties do you offer?
Every project includes our 10-year workmanship warranty covering installation defects. Additionally, as factory-certified installers, we register manufacturer warranties that range from 25 to 50 years depending on the selected system and product line.
Are your crews licensed and insured?
Absolutely. Structura Roofing Co. holds a valid Colorado roofing contractor license, carries $2 million in general liability insurance, and maintains full workers' compensation coverage for every crew member. Proof of insurance is available upon request.
