Our Story

From Blueprint to Roofline

Structura Roofing Co. was founded in Denver, Colorado, with a clear objective: bring structural engineering discipline to the roofing industry. Too many homeowners were dealing with vague estimates, inconsistent quality, and contractors who disappeared after the check cleared.

Our founder, a former structural engineer, saw an opportunity to apply systematic methodology to every phase of the roofing process. From the initial 28-point inspection protocol to the final documented walkthrough, every step follows a verified procedure designed for accountability and lasting performance.

Fifteen years later, that approach has earned the trust of over 2,800 residential and commercial clients across the Denver metro area. We hold factory certifications from GAF, Owens Corning, and CertainTeed, and every crew member is trained, licensed, and covered by our comprehensive insurance policy.
